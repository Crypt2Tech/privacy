Privacy Policy — C2T Tempo

Crypt2Tech · Last updated 18 September 2026

This policy explains how the Android application C2T Tempo (package com.crypt2tech.tempo), published by Crypt2Tech, handles your information.

The short version: C2T Tempo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal information. It has no account system and no sign-in.

Information the app sends

None. C2T Tempo has no internet connectivity of any kind. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. Nothing leaves your device because the app never makes a network connection.

Permissions

C2T Tempo requests no Android permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, storage, location, contacts, or calendar.

Information stored on your device

Your timer selection, custom duration, session count, and stopwatch laps exist only while the app is running, so they can survive events such as rotating the screen. This information is held by the app itself, is never transmitted anywhere, and is discarded when the app is closed.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share. No advertising networks, analytics providers, or other services are integrated into the app.

Children

C2T Tempo is suitable for users of all ages. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of C2T Tempo introduces features that handle data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change. We recommend reviewing this page after major updates.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about the app can be sent to crypt2tech@gmail.com.
